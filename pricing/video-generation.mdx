Video generation consumes **50 credits per minute**. It uses an existing Avatar and audio to produce a downloadable video.

## Credit estimates

| Video duration | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| 1 minute | 50 |
| 10 minutes | 500 |
| 60 minutes | 3,000 |

For budgeting, multiply the duration in minutes by 50. These examples illustrate the per-minute rate, not minimum charges or rounding rules.

See [Realtime Avatar Pricing](/pricing/realtime-avatar) for subscription prices and included credits, and the [Spatius pricing page](https://www.spatius.ai/pricing/) for current public plans. Realtime minute allowances shown on the pricing page are not video generation minute allowances.

## Avatar creation is separate

Generating a video does not reserve an Avatar Creation. If you need a new custom Avatar first, its [creation allowance or add-on charge](/pricing/custom-avatar-creation) is separate from the credits used to generate the video.

## Access and limits

The Video API is in private beta and requires separate access from the Avatar API. A credit balance alone does not enable video generation. Request access through [Spatius Studio feedback](https://app.spatius.ai/feedback/create).

Video API request limits are separate from credit consumption. See [Generate a Video](/api-reference/video-generation) for access requirements, request limits, and the create-and-download workflow.
