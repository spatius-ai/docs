Realtime avatar usage consumes **10 credits per minute**. For example, 100 minutes uses 1,000 credits.

All prices are in USD; pricing and limits may change during beta.

## Monthly billing

| Plan | Price per month | Included credits per month | Equivalent realtime minutes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 1,000 | 100 |
| Starter | $19 | 20,000 | 2,000 |
| Builder | $49 | 55,000 | 5,500 |
| Growth | $149 | 180,000 | 18,000 |
| Scale | $299 | 400,000 | 40,000 |

## Annual billing

Annual subscriptions are paid once per year at the rates below.

| Plan | Price per year | Included credits per year | Equivalent realtime minutes per year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $189 | 240,000 | 24,000 |
| Builder | $469 | 660,000 | 66,000 |
| Growth | $1,429 | 2,160,000 | 216,000 |
| Scale | $2,869 | 4,800,000 | 480,000 |

Minute equivalents assume the credits are used for realtime avatars. [Video generation](/pricing/video-generation) also consumes credits at its own per-minute rate.

## Session limits

| Plan | Concurrent sessions | Maximum session length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 2 | 10 minutes |
| Starter | 4 | 30 minutes |
| Builder | 8 | 60 minutes |
| Growth | 22 | 120 minutes |
| Scale | 40 | Unlimited |

Commercial usage is included on paid plans, not on Free. Custom avatar creation has [separate allowances and add-on pricing](/pricing/custom-avatar-creation).

## Additional usage

Paid plans can continue beyond included usage at these realtime per-minute rates:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter, Builder, Growth | $0.009/minute | $0.0072/minute |
| Scale | $0.007/minute | $0.0056/minute |

For Enterprise, contact sales for a volume-based contract, unlimited concurrent sessions, and dedicated support.
